YouTube Shorts captions for Indian creators

Short answer: Burn big, word-by-word captions into your Short so it works on mute, and upload a matching .srt in YouTube Studio for viewers who use CC. HandCut makes both on the iPhone, best on English speech, with a manual pass for Hindi or regional words.

Step by step

  1. Edit in 9:16. Import and set Canvas to 9:16.
  2. Tighten. Auto-Cut (Pro) removes silences before captioning.
  3. Caption. Generate Captions, then fix names and non-English words.
  4. Export video. Export Video for the burned-in version.
  5. Export SRT. Export .srt and add it in YouTube Studio under Subtitles.

Regional languages

Captions are built primarily for English speech. For Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi and other languages, HandCut can only caption if your iPhone has an on-device model for them; otherwise import an SRT made elsewhere with Import .srt and style it.

Readable on small screens

Many viewers watch on smaller phones, so keep two short lines and a high-contrast look. See captions too small.

Before you upload the Short

Export once, then open the file in YouTube's Shorts preview with the sound off. YouTube's interface covers the bottom of the frame and can crowd small captions. Keep two short lines in the safe zone, and use burned-in captions if you want them visible in the feed even when the viewer has captions turned off. The HandCut interface is in English. Other languages work only where your iPhone's on-device speech model is installed; otherwise fix the text or import an .srt file.
