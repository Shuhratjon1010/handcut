Reels captions for creators in India

Short answer: In India, short video means Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts; TikTok has been blocked since June 2020. On iPhone, HandCut makes burned-in, word-by-word captions on the phone. It works best on English speech, including Indian English, where your iPhone has the on-device model. Hindi and Hinglish need more manual fixes.

Step by step

  1. Shoot vertical. Record 9:16, or set Canvas to 9:16 after importing.
  2. Generate captions. Tap Generate Captions. If your language isn't available on-device, HandCut says so before generating.
  3. Fix the words. Tap any caption to correct names, places and Hindi words the recogniser misheard.
  4. Style it. Bold look, two lines at most, placed mid-frame above Instagram's username and caption area.
  5. Export and post. Export Video, then upload as a Reel and reuse it on Shorts.

About Hindi and Hinglish

Captions are built primarily for English speech. Hindi captions are only possible where your iPhone has an on-device Hindi speech model, and mixed Hindi-English sentences are hard for any single-language recogniser: expect to retype some words. See Hinglish captions for a workflow.

Data-light

Captions are made on the iPhone, so generating them uses no mobile data. Only downloading the app, buying the plan and uploading need a connection.

Questions people ask

Is TikTok available in India?

No. The Government of India blocked TikTok in June 2020, and the block remains in force.

Can I reuse the same video on Shorts?

Yes. A 9:16 export with burned-in captions works on Reels and Shorts.
