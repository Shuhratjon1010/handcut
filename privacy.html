Privacy Policy — Handcut

Effective 1 August 2026.

The short version

Handcut collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no server belonging to us for your video to be sent to. Editing, captioning and exporting all happen on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Our App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected”. That is a statement about what the app is capable of, not about what we choose to do: the app links no networking framework and contains no networking code outside Apple’s own StoreKit.

Your video, audio and captions

Your footage never leaves your device. Captions are produced by Apple’s on-device speech recognition with network recognition explicitly disabled and no fallback path — the app transcribes in Airplane Mode, which is the simplest way to see this for yourself.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether a purchase is active; nothing identifying you reaches us, because there is no “us” to reach.

When you share a video

Using the share sheet, or sending a video to another app, hands the file to software we do not control. Once it leaves Handcut it is governed by that app’s privacy policy, not this one.

Children

Handcut collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be dated here. A change that introduced data collection would require a new App Store privacy label, which is visible on the listing before you install.
