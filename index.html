HandCut: auto captions that land on the word

HandCut is a captions-first video editor for iPhone. It transcribes your video on the device and times every caption to the spoken word. No account, no ads, and never a watermark.

Edit free. Export with Pro, a monthly subscription. Price shown in the app.

What you get

  • Word-by-word, karaoke-style auto captions; edit any word, restyle, move them anywhere on the frame
  • Burn captions into the video, or export an SRT file
  • Picture-in-picture: screen recording plus a facecam bubble plus captions
  • A real editor: multi-clip timeline, trim, split, reorder, 14 transitions, 0.25x to 4x speed, music and voiceover mixer, manual keyframes, reframe to vertical, square or widescreen
  • Export up to 4K, up to 60 fps, matching your footage

Free vs Pro

Download and editing are free. Exporting needs HandCut Pro, a monthly subscription; the App Store price for your country is shown in the app before you buy. Pro also includes tools such as transcript editing (delete words to cut video), motion tracking, Smart Cutout, Auto-Reframe, denoise, Auto Match Look and version history. Existing purchases can be restored.

FAQ

Does HandCut add a watermark?

No. HandCut never adds a watermark to any export.

Is HandCut free?

HandCut is free to download and free to edit with. Exporting a video needs a HandCut Pro monthly subscription; the price is shown in the app before you buy. Existing purchases can be restored in the app.

Do I need an account?

No. HandCut asks for no account and shows no ads.

How are captions made?

Apple's on-device speech recognition transcribes your video on your iPhone, and HandCut times each caption to the spoken word. Audio is not uploaded.

Which languages are supported?

Captions work in the languages your iPhone can recognise offline; the export screen lists them. English is the most tested. The app interface is available in English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Hindi and Indonesian.

Is HandCut on Android?

No. HandCut is iPhone only, iOS 17 or later.
