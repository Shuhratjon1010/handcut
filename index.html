How to add captions to a video on iPhone

  1. Open or record a clip. Pick a video from your library, or record with the teleprompter camera.
  2. Generate captions. Speech recognition runs on your iPhone and times each word.
  3. Fix any word. Edit the transcript directly; the captions follow.
  4. Choose a look and export. Export up to 4K, with no watermark.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add captions to a video on iPhone?

Open the app, pick a clip from your library or record one with the built-in teleprompter camera, and generate captions. They are created on your iPhone with Apple's on-device speech recognition and highlight each word as it is spoken. Tap any word in the transcript to correct it, choose a caption look, and export.

Is Handcut free?

Yes, it is a free download. The free plan includes 4K export, no watermark, unlimited exports without captions, timeline editing, keyframes, green screen, picture-in-picture tracks, the teleprompter camera and caption generation, plus 3 exports with captions in any rolling 7 days. Pro, a subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, adds unlimited captioned exports, Magic Edit and more.

Is there a watermark on exported videos?

No. There is no watermark on any plan, including the free plan.

Does it work offline?

Captions are generated by Apple's on-device speech recognition on your iPhone, not by an online service, so your footage is not uploaded to be captioned and no account is needed. It depends on iOS having the on-device speech model for your language on your iPhone.

Which languages can it caption?

English is the primary language. Other languages work where iOS provides an on-device speech model for that language on your iPhone, so availability varies by language and device. On iOS 18 or later, Pro can also translate captions on device and show bilingual captions.

How do I caption a talking-head video?

Record with the teleprompter camera so you can read your script near the lens, then generate word-by-word captions and fix any word in the transcript. With Pro, Magic Edit removes filler words and long silences, captions, reframes and adds emphasis motion in one tap.

Does it have a teleprompter?

Yes, and it is free. The teleprompter camera scrolls your script near the lens while you record, so you can read it while keeping your eyes close to the camera.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login, and the app shows no ads.
