How HandCut's caption generator for video works

Short answer: HandCut generates captions from your video's speech on the iPhone, keeps each word's timing for karaoke-style highlighting, lets you edit and style every caption, and exports with captions burned in and no watermark. There's no account and no ads.

How it works

When you tap Generate Captions, HandCut sends the audio to Apple's speech recogniser with on-device recognition required, so nothing goes to a server. The recogniser returns text in segments with a start time and duration for each word. HandCut groups those words into short caption lines and keeps every word's timing, which is what makes word-by-word and karaoke looks possible. For the product overview, see the HandCut auto captions app.

What accuracy depends on

  • Mic distance: the closer the better.
  • Background sound: music and crowds compete with speech.
  • Echo: bare rooms blur consonants.
  • Vocabulary: names, brands and jargon are the usual misses; fix them once with find and replace.

What you get

  • One-tap Generate Captions using Apple's on-device speech recognition, with no server fallback.
  • Per-word timing for word-by-word and karaoke looks.
  • Caption presets, looks and text animations, all included.
  • Edit any word; find and replace across the transcript.
  • Burned-in export up to 4K, or an .srt caption file.
  • Pro tools: Auto-Cut (silences, filler words), transcript editing, Hook title, auto emoji, caption translation.

Who it's for

People who talk to camera: coaches, course creators, realtors, podcasters and small businesses.

Limits, stated plainly

  • iPhone only, iOS 17 or later. No Android, web or desktop.
  • HandCut's captions are built primarily for English speech. Other languages work only when your iPhone has an on-device speech model for them, and the app tells you before you generate if yours does not.
  • No templates, stock library or AI avatars.
