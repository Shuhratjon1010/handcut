A private, on-device video editor for iPhone

Short answer: HandCut has no account, shows no ads, and makes captions with Apple's on-device speech recognition only. Its App Store privacy label says "Data Not Collected". Your footage stays on your iPhone unless you share it.

What runs on the iPhone

  • Caption transcription (no server fallback)
  • Caption translation via Apple's on-device Translation framework
  • Person detection for Auto-Reframe, tracking and Smart Cutout (Apple Vision)
  • Rendering and export

How competitors label it

On the US App Store, the privacy labels for CapCut, InShot, VN and Captions each include a "Data Used to Track You" section (read 18 November 2026). HandCut's says "Data Not Collected". Privacy labels are self-reported by developers; read each app's policy for details.

Who cares about this

Therapists, coaches and doctors recording educational clips, parents filming kids, lawyers and HR teams, journalists, and anyone who'd rather not upload raw footage to a server.

Download HandCut on the App Store

Download is free, and editing and preview are free. For new users, exporting needs the HandCut monthly plan (the price is shown in the app), which includes every Pro tool. No credits, no ads, no account. Cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings. If you installed HandCut before version 4.3, you keep the access you were promised, and past purchases can be restored in the app.

Questions people ask

Does HandCut collect my data?

Its App Store privacy label states data is not collected. The privacy policy has the details.

Does the paywall need internet?

Buying or restoring the plan goes through the App Store, which needs a connection.
