Automatic captions are produced by Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio is not uploaded, and there is no network fallback — if the language is not available offline on your device, the feature reports that rather than sending your audio somewhere.

Recognition accuracy varies with audio quality, accent and language. Captions are a starting point you are expected to edit, not a transcript we warrant to be correct.
