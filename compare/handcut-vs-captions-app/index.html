HandCut vs Captions: AI studio or on-device captions?

Short answer: Captions (by Captions, LLC, built by Mirage) is an AI video studio: AI edit, AI avatars, dubbing into 29 languages, subtitles in 91+ languages and a teleprompter. HandCut does much less, on purpose: captions transcribed on your iPhone, a real timeline, no account, no ads and no watermark.

Pick Captions if

  • You need many caption or dubbing languages
  • You want AI to edit for you or an AI avatar

Pick HandCut if

  • You want to edit yourself, with captions made on the phone
  • You want no account and a Data Not Collected label
  • You want a simple monthly plan with no credits

Download is free, and editing and preview are free. For new users, exporting needs the HandCut monthly plan (the price is shown in the app), which includes every Pro tool. Questions people ask

Is HandCut the same as the Captions app?

No. They're separate apps from separate developers.

Which is better for non-English captions?

Captions lists 91+ subtitle languages. HandCut is built primarily for English; you can import an SRT made elsewhere.
