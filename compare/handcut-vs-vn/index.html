HandCut vs VN: an honest comparison

Short answer: VN is a strong, free editor with no watermark, multi-track editing, masks, LUTs, templates, auto captions with filler and silence removal, and a teleprompter. If price is the deciding factor, VN wins. HandCut is narrower: word-by-word captions transcribed on the iPhone, no account, no ads, and a Data Not Collected privacy label.

Pick VN if

  • You want a free editor
  • You need masks, LUTs, HDR or templates
  • You edit on iPad too

Pick HandCut if

  • You care most about captions made on your phone
  • You want no account and no tracking per the App Store label
  • You prefer a small, focused app

Download HandCut on the App Store

Download is free, and editing and preview are free. For new users, exporting needs the HandCut monthly plan (the price is shown in the app), which includes every Pro tool. No credits, no ads, no account. Cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings. If you installed HandCut before version 4.3, you keep the access you were promised, and past purchases can be restored in the app.

Questions people ask

Is VN really free with no watermark?

That's what VN's App Store listing says. It also offers in-app purchases.

Why pay for HandCut if VN is free?

If VN does what you need, use it. HandCut is for people who want captions-first editing on-device, with no account or ads.
