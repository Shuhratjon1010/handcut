HandCut vs InShot: which should you use?

Short answer: InShot is a mature all-round editor with auto and bilingual captions, AI cut, stickers and a photo editor and collage maker. Its terms say the subscription removes the InShot logo and ads. HandCut is a captions-first editor that never adds a watermark, shows no ads, and transcribes on the iPhone.

Pick InShot if

  • You also edit photos and collages
  • You want stickers, templates and bilingual subtitles
  • You edit on iPad or Android too

Pick HandCut if

  • Your videos are you talking to camera and captions matter most
  • You want captions made on the phone, no account and no ads
  • You never want to think about a watermark

Questions people ask

Does InShot add a watermark?

InShot's terms say a subscription includes removal of the InShot logo and ads, so free exports can carry the logo.

Which has better captions?

Both generate captions automatically. InShot offers bilingual subtitles; HandCut focuses on word-by-word timing made on-device.
