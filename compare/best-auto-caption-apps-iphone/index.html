The best auto caption apps for iPhone, compared

Short answer: There isn't one best app. Captions leads on languages and AI. CapCut and InShot are all-round editors with captions built in. VN is free with no watermark. HandCut is the pick if you want word-by-word captions made on the iPhone itself, with no account and no ads.
