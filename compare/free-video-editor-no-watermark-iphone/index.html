Free video editors with no watermark on iPhone: an honest list

Short answer: Free and no watermark: VN (its App Store listing says "Free with No Watermark") and Apple's iMovie. CapCut exports are usually watermark-free, but templates can add a branded ending clip. InShot's terms say the subscription removes its logo. HandCut never adds a watermark, but it isn't free to export for new users.

So why does HandCut charge?

HandCut is a small, captions-first app with no ads, no account, no tracking per its App Store privacy label, and captions made on the iPhone. It's paid by a single monthly plan instead of ads or data. If you don't need that, the free apps above are good choices. If you do, see HandCut's no-watermark editor.

How to check any app for a watermark

  1. Export a 5-second test clip before you edit for real.
  2. Check the corners and the last two seconds for an outro.
  3. Try one template and one premium-looking effect, since those are where watermarks hide.
