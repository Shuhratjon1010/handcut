CapCut alternatives for iPhone

Short answer: If you want free with no watermark, try VN. For an all-rounder with a photo editor, InShot. For AI editing and many caption languages, Captions. For private, word-by-word captions on talking video, HandCut. And Apple's iMovie is free for simple edits, without auto captions.

The picks

  1. VN: listing says "Free with No Watermark"; deep multi-track timeline, masks, LUTs, templates, auto captions, filler and silence removal, teleprompter.
  2. InShot: all-round editor plus photo editor and collage maker; auto and bilingual captions, AI Cut. Terms say the subscription removes the logo and ads.
  3. Captions: AI editing, avatars, dubbing in 29 languages, subtitles in 91+ languages.
  4. HandCut: word-by-word captions made on the iPhone, no account, no ads, never a watermark; monthly plan for new users to export.
  5. iMovie: Apple's free editor for simple timelines; no auto captions.

Why people leave CapCut

Common reasons in public App Store reviews include paywalled features, template watermarks and server-side tools being busy. CapCut remains the most feature-rich mobile editor; switching makes sense if you only use part of it.
