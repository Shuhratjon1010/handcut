HandCut vs CapCut: which is better for captions?

Short answer: CapCut is the bigger, more capable editor, with templates, a huge effects and music library, desktop and web versions. HandCut is narrower: a small iPhone app for word-by-word captions made on-device, with no account, no ads and never a watermark. Choose CapCut for templates and effects; choose HandCut for private, fast captions on talking video.

Where CapCut wins

  • Templates and trends, effects, stickers and a large music library
  • Desktop and browser versions
  • Wider speed range and more AI tools
  • A huge user base and tutorials

Where HandCut wins

  • Captions are transcribed on the iPhone with no server fallback
  • No watermark logic to work around, ever
  • No account, no ads, and a privacy label of Data Not Collected
  • A small app (about 10 MB) focused on talking-to-camera video

Download HandCut on the App Store

Download is free, and editing and preview are free. For new users, exporting needs the HandCut monthly plan (the price is shown in the app), which includes every Pro tool. No credits, no ads, no account. Cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings. If you installed HandCut before version 4.3, you keep the access you were promised, and past purchases can be restored in the app.

Questions people ask

Is HandCut a CapCut alternative?

For captioning talking videos on iPhone, yes. As a full replacement for CapCut's templates and effects library, no.

Does CapCut add a watermark?

According to CapCut's help pages, not usually on export; the watermark generally comes from a template's branded ending clip, which you can delete.
