How to add subtitles to a video on iPhone

You can add subtitles to a video on iPhone without a computer. With HandCut the subtitles are generated on the device and timed to each word.

  1. Install HandCut from the App Store and open it. No account is needed.
  2. Tap the button to pick a video from Photos.
  3. Open Captions and generate them. HandCut transcribes the speech on your iPhone and times each word.
  4. Tap any word to fix it, choose a caption style, and drag the captions where you want them.
  5. Tap Export. Captions are burned into the video, with no watermark. Exporting needs HandCut Pro; the price is shown in the app.

Tips for better subtitles

  • Record close to the microphone and away from loud music; recognition is better with clean speech.
  • Keep two short lines on screen at most so viewers can read them at a glance.
  • Place subtitles above the bottom edge so app buttons on social platforms do not cover them.

Burned-in subtitles or an SRT file

Burned-in subtitles become part of the picture, so they show on every platform even when the viewer has captions turned off. An SRT file stays editable and is what YouTube and many course platforms want you to upload. You can export both from the same project. For languages, translation and safe zones, see the complete subtitles guide.

The app interface is in English. Captions are built for English; other languages work only where your iPhone's on-device speech model is installed.
