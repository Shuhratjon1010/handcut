Handcut vs CapCut for captions

CapCut is the bigger editor, with templates and trending effects on more platforms. Pick CapCut if you build from templates. Pick Handcut if you mainly need word-by-word captions on talking-to-camera video on your iPhone, with no watermark to remove.
