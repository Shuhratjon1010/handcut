An auto captions app for iPhone that times every word

Most people watch short video with the sound off. HandCut turns speech into captions on your iPhone, word by word, so the text appears exactly as each word is spoken.

Edit free. Export with Pro, a monthly subscription. Price shown in the app.

How HandCut's auto captions work

  • On-device transcription. Apple's speech recognition runs on your iPhone. Your audio is not uploaded.
  • Word timing. Each caption follows the timing of the spoken words, with karaoke-style highlighting.
  • Edit and style. Fix any word, change the look, and place captions anywhere on the frame.
  • Export. Burn captions into the video with no watermark, or export SRT.

Who it is for

Coaches, course creators, realtors, podcasters and anyone talking to camera who wants captions without a desktop editor.

Languages

Captions are built for English. Other languages work only where your iPhone's on-device speech model supports them, and the export screen lists the ones your iPhone has.

FAQ

Are the auto captions accurate?

Accuracy depends on audio quality, accent and language. HandCut uses Apple's on-device recognition; you can tap any word to correct it before export.

Do auto captions work offline?

Transcription runs on your iPhone with no network fallback. If a language is not available offline on your iPhone, HandCut tells you before you start.

Can I export the captions as a file?

Yes. You can burn captions into the video or export an SRT file.

Does HandCut add a watermark?

No. HandCut never adds a watermark to any export.

Is HandCut free?

HandCut is free to download and free to edit with. Exporting a video needs a HandCut Pro monthly subscription; the price is shown in the app before you buy. Existing purchases can be restored in the app.
