Frequently asked

What is free, and what needs Pro?

Downloading and editing are free. Exporting needs HandCut Pro, a monthly subscription; the App Store price for your country is shown in the app before you buy. Pro also adds tools such as transcript editing, motion tracking, Smart Cutout, Auto-Reframe, denoise, Auto Match Look and version history. If you had HandCut before the 4.3 update, you keep the editor you already had.

I already paid. How do I get Pro back?

Tap Restore purchases in the app. Earlier subscriptions and lifetime purchases stay valid. Manage subscriptions in Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Does HandCut add a watermark?

No. Never, on any export.

Do captions work offline?

Transcription runs on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition, with no network fallback. Availability depends on the languages iOS has installed; the export screen lists the languages your iPhone supports offline, and HandCut says so before you start if a language is missing.

Why is there no music library?

Licensing one would mean a server, accounts and a very different app. You can add your own audio.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or password.

Where do my projects live? Do they sync?

Your media never leaves your device. The small project file describing your cuts syncs through your own iCloud backup, which we cannot access. The media itself is excluded from that backup and can be re-imported from Photos.
