Frequently asked

What do I get without paying?

4K export, keyframe animation, two video tracks so picture-in-picture works, three caption styles, and no watermark — on every tier, always. Projects and video length are unlimited, and so are exports without burned-in captions. The free plan includes 3 captioned exports per week; Pro removes that limit. Resolution is not the paywall here, and neither is basic editing capability.

What does Pro add?

Green-screen removal, additional video layers, adjustment layers and the full caption style library. Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock: $9.99 in the US (the app shows the exact price in your region's currency before you buy). Pay once, own it forever — no subscription required. Already bought Pro or subscribed earlier? Tap Restore Purchases in the app; existing subscriptions keep working and can be managed in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Changed 23 August 2026: 4K export and keyframe animation used to be Pro. They are free now, permanently, for everyone — including anyone already subscribed. Nothing was taken away and nothing needs to be bought back; the page said otherwise until this date, and this line is here because a correction that is not visible is not a correction.

Do captions work offline?

Yes. Transcription runs on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device speech recognition, with no network fallback. The app links no networking code at all, so there is nothing to send your audio with. Availability depends on the language models iOS has installed on your device; if a language has no on-device model, Handcut says so before you start rather than failing partway.

Why is there no music library?

Because licensing one would mean a server, accounts and a very different app. You can add your own audio. We would rather be honest about the trade than pretend otherwise.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no password, because there is nowhere for an account to live.

Where do my projects live? Do they sync?

Your media never leaves your device. Your project structure — the small file describing your cuts and settings — syncs through your own iCloud backup, which we have no access to, so a new iPhone can restore your work.

The media itself is deliberately excluded from that backup: a project's clips can be gigabytes, and they can be re-imported from Photos, so backing them up would consume your iCloud storage for no real benefit.
