Frequently asked

What do I get without paying?

1080p export, two video tracks so picture-in-picture works, three caption styles, and no watermark — on every tier, always. Projects, exports and video length are unlimited and always have been.

What does Pro add?

Keyframe animation, green-screen removal, additional video layers, 4K export and the full caption style library. Pro is $4.99 monthly or $29.99 a year — roughly six months’ price for twelve — with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan. There is also a one-time $69.99 lifetime purchase if you would rather not subscribe.

Do captions work offline?

Yes. Transcription runs on your iPhone using Apple’s on-device speech recognition, with no network fallback, so it works in Airplane Mode and your audio is never uploaded. Availability depends on the language models iOS has installed on your device; if a language has no on-device model, Handcut says so before you start rather than failing partway.

Why is there no music library?

Because licensing one would mean a server, accounts and a very different app. You can add your own audio. We would rather be honest about the trade than pretend otherwise.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no password, because there is nowhere for an account to live. Your projects are on your device.
