How to add captions to a video on iPhone

This is the short version: import a talking-head clip, generate word-by-word captions on your iPhone, fix any word, pick a style, and export with no watermark. For burned-in versus SRT files, languages and translation, see the complete subtitles guide.

  1. Install HandCut from the App Store and open it. No account is needed.
  2. Tap the button to pick a video from Photos.
  3. Open Captions and generate them. HandCut transcribes the speech on your iPhone and times each word.
  4. Tap any word to fix it, choose a caption style, and drag the captions where you want them.
  5. Tap Export. Captions are burned into the video, with no watermark. Exporting needs HandCut Pro; the price is shown in the app.

Make the captions easier to read

  • Tap any word that came out wrong. Names and mixed languages often need a quick fix.
  • Keep two short lines on screen, and sit the captions above the bottom of the frame so app buttons do not cover them.
  • Preview the export on mute. If you cannot follow the video from the text alone, enlarge the style or shorten the lines.

Captions are built for English. Other languages work only where your iPhone's on-device speech model is installed; the export screen lists the ones your iPhone has. The app itself is in English.
