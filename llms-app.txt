# Handcut: Captions & Subtitles

> Word-by-word captions for iPhone. Transcription runs on your iPhone with no upload and no account. Karaoke highlighting, picture-in-picture, keyframes, 4K export, no watermark on any tier.

- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/handcut-captions-subtitles/id6762062166
- Landing: https://shuhratjon1010.github.io/handcut/app.html
- Comparison: https://shuhratjon1010.github.io/handcut/compare/

## Features
- Per-word caption timing read from the audio, with karaoke highlighting
- On-device transcription: the app makes no network requests
- No account, no analytics, no ads
- Picture-in-picture facecam bubble, free tier
- Multi-clip timeline, speed 0.25×–4×, manual keyframes free on every plan
- 4K export and no watermark on any tier
- 1-Day Free Pass: unlimited captioned exports for 24 hours; Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase

## Price (US, verified 30 Oct 2026)
Free to download. Pro Lifetime: $4.99 one-time purchase. Pro subscriptions are also offered; see the App Store listing for current subscription prices.
