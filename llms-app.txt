# HandCut: Auto Captions Video

> Word-by-word captions for iPhone. Transcription runs on your iPhone with no upload and no account. Karaoke highlighting, picture-in-picture, keyframes, 4K export, never a watermark. Edit free; exporting needs HandCut Pro, a monthly subscription priced in the app.
