Caption safe zones for TikTok, Reels and Shorts

Short answer: On a 9:16 video, keep captions in the middle band of the frame, away from the bottom fifth and the right edge. All three apps draw usernames, post text and buttons there. The top strip is also busy with tabs and search.

A simple rule of thumb

  • Top ~12%: app tabs and search. Keep hook titles just below it.
  • Middle: the safe band for captions and on-screen text.
  • Bottom ~20–25%: username, caption text, music label. Avoid.
  • Right ~15%: like, comment, share, remix buttons. Avoid.

The exact sizes change when the apps update, so preview your export on each platform before a big post.

How HandCut places text

Captions start a little below the centre of the frame, and a hook title sits near the top, below the tab bar. You can drag either anywhere.

Size and contrast

People watch on small screens in bright light. Use a bold look with an outline or background bar, and keep lines short.

Check before you post

Export once, then open the file in each app's upload preview before publishing. If the username or buttons touch your text, move the captions up a little and export again. It takes a minute and saves a reposted video.

Questions people ask

Are the safe zones the same on TikTok and Reels?

Close enough that one placement usually works for both. Shorts has a slightly different bottom bar.

Should captions go at the top instead?

Top placement fights the app tabs and the viewer's eye line. Middle or just below centre works best for talking heads.
