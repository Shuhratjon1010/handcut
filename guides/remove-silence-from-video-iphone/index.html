How to remove silence from a video on iPhone

Short answer: Use HandCut's Auto-Cut (a Pro tool): it finds the quiet gaps between your sentences and removes them to tighten pacing. You can review what it cut before you export.

Long pauses are the main reason talking-to-camera clips feel slow. Cutting them by hand means scrubbing the waveform and splitting dozens of times. Auto-Cut does the first pass for you.

Step by step

  1. Import the clip. Tap Add Video. One long take works best.
  2. Open Auto-Cut. Choose Auto-Cut and the option to Remove silences and tighten pacing.
  3. Review. Play it back. If a cut feels abrupt, undo it or adjust the clip edges on the timeline.
  4. Caption after cutting. Generate captions after the cuts so they match the final timing.
  5. Export. Tap Export Video.

How it decides what's silent

Auto-Cut measures loudness across the audio and treats sustained quiet sections as gaps. Background noise, music or a fan can hide pauses, so record in a quiet room or use Reduce Noise first if the result leaves gaps in.

Filler words are a separate step

Silence and "um" are different problems. To find filler words, see removing filler words.

Questions people ask

Is there a free way to remove silence on iPhone?

You can split and delete pauses by hand in most editors. Automatic silence removal in HandCut is a Pro tool; new users get it in the monthly plan they need to export.

Will cutting silence make jump cuts?

Yes, every removed gap is a cut. On talking-head video that's normal and keeps pace up. Captions and a slight zoom on some cuts can smooth it.
