How to translate captions on iPhone

Short answer: After generating captions, tap Translate captions (a Pro tool). HandCut uses Apple's on-device translation, available on iOS 18 or later, and keeps your original captions so you can switch back at any time.

Step by step

  1. Generate captions in the spoken language. Tap Generate Captions and fix the text first. A clean source makes a better translation.
  2. Translate. Tap Translate captions and choose the target language. iOS may ask you to download that language the first time.
  3. Review. Machine translation can miss idioms. Read the result and edit any line.
  4. Export. Tap Export Video to burn the translated captions in, or Export .srt for a subtitle file.

Your originals stay

Translating doesn't destroy the source captions. You can show the original again, and switch between languages you've already translated. If a subscription lapses, translations you already made stay usable.

Speech vs translation languages

Recognising speech and translating text are different steps. HandCut's captions are built primarily for English speech. Other languages work only when your iPhone has an on-device speech model for them, and the app tells you before you generate if yours does not. Translation then turns that text into another language.

Questions people ask

Does translation upload my captions?

No. It runs through Apple's on-device Translation framework.

Which iOS do I need?

Caption translation needs iOS 18 or later. Captions themselves work from iOS 17.
