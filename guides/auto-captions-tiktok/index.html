How to add auto captions to TikTok videos

Short answer: TikTok's own auto captions can be switched off by viewers and use a fixed style. For captions that are always visible and styled your way, generate them in HandCut, place them in the middle of the 9:16 frame away from TikTok's buttons, and export with the captions burned in.

TikTok offers built-in auto captions when you post, and they're fine for accessibility. Creators burn captions in for a different reason: most people scroll with the sound off, and big, word-by-word text in a style you chose keeps them watching. Here's the workflow in HandCut.

Step by step

  1. Set the canvas to 9:16. Open Canvas and choose 9:16. If your clip was filmed sideways, fill the frame or add a blurred background so nothing gets cut off.
  2. Generate captions. Tap Generate Captions. Each word keeps its own timing, so a word-by-word or karaoke look stays in sync.
  3. Pick a bold look. In Style, choose a high-contrast preset. Short lines read faster on a phone, so keep two lines at most.
  4. Keep them in the safe area. Drag the captions to roughly the middle of the frame. TikTok draws the caption text, username and buttons over the bottom and right edges.
  5. Export and upload. Tap Export Video. The file saves to Photos and you upload it in TikTok as usual.

Burned-in captions vs TikTok's caption toggle

TikTok's generated captions are a layer the viewer can hide. Burned-in captions are pixels in the video, so they always show and look the same everywhere the clip is reposted. If you burn captions in, turn TikTok's auto captions off when you post so viewers don't see two sets.

Make the first two seconds count

A short title over the opening line helps people decide to stay. HandCut's Hook title (a Pro tool) lifts the first thing you say from the captions and puts it on screen for a couple of seconds. See adding a hook title.

Questions people ask

Why do my TikTok captions get covered?

The bottom of the screen holds your caption text and the right side holds the like, comment and share buttons. Keep burned-in captions near the middle.

What resolution should I export for TikTok?

1080×1920 is the common choice. HandCut can export up to 4K if your footage and iPhone support it.

Does HandCut post to TikTok for me?

No. It saves the video to Photos and you upload it in TikTok.
