How to fix and edit auto captions on iPhone

Short answer: Tap any caption in HandCut to edit its text. Your fix keeps the word timing, so highlights still land on time. For a name that's wrong everywhere, use find and replace in the transcript instead of fixing it line by line.

The usual mistakes

  • Names and brands: the recogniser guesses a common word. Fix once, then replace everywhere.
  • Numbers: "twenty twenty six" vs "2026". Pick one style.
  • Homophones: their/there, your/you're.
  • Missing punctuation: add a full stop where you pause for a cleaner line break.

Edit a single caption

Select the caption on the timeline or the preview and change the text. Restyling and moving it happens in Style and by dragging on the frame.

Fix the whole video at once

Use find and replace in the transcript to swap every "hand cut" for "HandCut", for example. It's quicker and avoids missing one.

Get fewer mistakes next time

Speak clearly, record close to the mic, and reduce background noise. Clear audio is the biggest accuracy gain for any speech recogniser.

Splitting long captions

Captions that run three or four lines are hard to read on a phone. Shorter caption groups (two lines, five to eight words) read faster and suit word-by-word looks. Adding punctuation where you pause also helps lines break in natural places.

Questions people ask

Will editing a word change when it appears?

No. Editing text keeps the timing of the words you're fixing.

Can I delete words to cut the video?

Yes, with transcript editing, a Pro tool: deleting words in the transcript removes that part of the video.
