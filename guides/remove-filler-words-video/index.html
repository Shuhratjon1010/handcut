How to remove filler words like "um" and "uh" from a video

Short answer: Generate captions first, then use Identify filler words to mark the ums and uhs in the transcript. Because the captions know each word's timing, HandCut can cut them from the video (filler removal is part of Pro).

Step by step

  1. Generate captions. Filler detection reads the transcript, so tap Generate Captions first.
  2. Identify filler words. In the captions view, tap Identify filler words. HandCut highlights them, or says No filler words found.
  3. Cut them. Send the marked words to Auto-Cut to remove them from the video.
  4. Listen once. Removing a filler mid-sentence can leave a hard cut. Undo any that sound worse than the um.
  5. Export. Tap Export Video.

Why the transcript makes this easy

On a waveform, "um" looks like any other word. In a transcript with word timings it's just a word you can find. The same idea powers transcript editing, where deleting words in the text cuts the video.

Languages

Filler lists exist for several languages, but caption quality matters most, and captions are built primarily for English speech.

Questions people ask

Should I remove every filler word?

Not always. A few keep speech natural. Remove the ones that stall a sentence.

Does it remove 'like' and 'you know'?

Those can be real words, so check each one before cutting.
