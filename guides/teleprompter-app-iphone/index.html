How to use a teleprompter on iPhone and caption the take

Short answer: HandCut has a built-in Teleprompter: paste your script, set scroll speed and text size, and record with the front camera while it scrolls. Keep the take, and it goes straight into the editor for captions.

Step by step

  1. Open Teleprompter. From the HandCut home screen, choose Teleprompter and allow camera and microphone access.
  2. Add the script. Paste your script or type it in.
  3. Set speed and size. Adjust scroll speed and text size so you read at a natural pace.
  4. Record. Look at the lens, read, and stop when you're done. Retake if needed.
  5. Use the take. Choose the take and it opens in the editor. Then Generate Captions and export.

Tips for sounding natural

Write the way you talk, in short sentences. Put a line break where you want to breathe. Keep the phone at eye level so your eyes don't drift down to the text.

Why teleprompter and captions belong together

A scripted take has fewer fillers and pauses, so captions need fewer fixes and Auto-Cut has less to remove.

Troubleshooting

  • Teleprompter is blocked: allow camera and microphone access in iOS Settings, then open it again.
  • Recording interrupted: a call or alarm stops the take. Record the take again.
  • Reading looks obvious: lower the text size so your eyes move less, and slow the scroll.
