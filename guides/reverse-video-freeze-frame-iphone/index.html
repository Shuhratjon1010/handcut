How to reverse a clip and add a freeze frame on iPhone

Short answer: In HandCut, select a video clip and choose Reverse to play it backwards, or move the playhead to a moment and choose Freeze to hold that frame. Both work on the timeline like any other clip.

Step by step

  1. Reverse. Select the clip, tap Reverse, and wait while HandCut renders the reversed copy. Only video clips can be reversed.
  2. Freeze frame. Put the playhead on the moment, then tap Freeze. A held frame is added.
  3. Trim the freeze. Drag its edge to set how long it holds.
  4. Add text. A freeze frame is a natural place for a title or caption.
  5. Export. Tap Export Video.

Ideas

  • Freeze on the punchline and add a caption.
  • Reverse a spill, a jump or a throw for a hook.
  • Freeze before a transformation, then cut to the result.

Audio

Reversed speech sounds like noise, so mute the reversed clip or put music under it. A freeze frame has no live audio; let music or a voiceover carry it.

Ideas that read clearly

A short reverse is enough: a product assembling, a message appearing, a jump landing. Long reversed speech becomes hard to watch and the captions will not make sense unless you write new ones. A freeze frame works well as a hold on a key price, name or step. Place it, add a short title if needed, then continue. Neither effect needs a watermark, and you can combine them with the rest of the timeline as usual.

Questions people ask

Why can't I reverse a clip?

Only video can be reversed, and one clip at a time. HandCut tells you if a reverse is already running.
