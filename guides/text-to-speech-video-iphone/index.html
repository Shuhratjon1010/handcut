How to add a text-to-speech voiceover on iPhone

Short answer: Open Text to Speech in HandCut, type or paste your script, pick one of the on-device voice presets, and add the generated audio to the timeline. It uses Apple's speech synthesis on the iPhone, so the text isn't uploaded.

Step by step

  1. Open Text to Speech. From the editor's audio tools.
  2. Enter the script. Short sentences sound more natural.
  3. Pick a voice preset. Presets are neutral styles (natural, warm, bright, calm, narrator) with different pacing.
  4. Add it to the timeline. Place the voice clip under your footage, and turn on ducking if there's music.
  5. Caption it. Generate captions from the voiceover so the words appear on screen.

When text to speech helps

  • You don't want your own voice on camera.
  • Quick product demos and app walkthroughs.
  • Translating a short script into a voice for another audience.

Limits

Voices depend on the speech voices installed on your iPhone. Synthetic voices can sound flat on long scripts; for brand content, a recorded voiceover usually sounds better. HandCut's presets use neutral names, never real people's.
