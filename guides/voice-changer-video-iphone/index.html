How to use a voice changer on iPhone video

Short answer: Select a clip or voiceover in HandCut and open Voice Changer. Pick an effect such as studio, deep, high, robot, echo, megaphone, telephone, vinyl, monster, synth or whisper, preview it, and export. Effects run on the iPhone.

Step by step

  1. Select the audio. A video clip with speech or a recorded voiceover.
  2. Open Voice Changer. From the audio tools.
  3. Pick an effect. Preview a few; telephone and megaphone suit comedy, deep or high can disguise a voice.
  4. Check captions. Captions are made from the audio, so generate them before applying heavy effects.
  5. Export. Tap Export Video.

Keep it understandable

Strong effects make speech harder to follow, especially on phone speakers. Use them for short moments, and keep captions on so the words are never lost.

Disguising a voice

A pitch effect is not real anonymity: voices can sometimes be recognised or reversed. For sensitive interviews, consider a text-to-speech re-voice and blur the face with Censor.

Troubleshooting

  • The effect is hard to understand: pick a milder effect, or apply it only to a short line, not the whole take.
  • Captions no longer match: generate or fix captions after you choose the effect, using the words you actually want on screen, not a garbled playback.
  • Only one clip sounds changed: effects are per clip or voiceover. Apply the same effect to each clip you want to match.
