How to extract audio from a video on iPhone

Short answer: Select the clip in HandCut and choose Extract Audio. The sound becomes its own audio clip on the timeline, so you can move, trim or reuse it independently of the picture, for example keeping your voice under B-roll.

Step by step

  1. Select the clip. Tap the video on the timeline.
  2. Extract. Choose Extract Audio. An audio clip appears under the video.
  3. Use it. Replace the picture with B-roll while the voice continues, or move the audio for J and L cuts.
  4. Mix. Adjust levels in the mixer; mute the original if needed.
  5. Caption. Captions follow speech wherever it sits on the timeline.

Common uses

  • Talking-head audio over screen recordings or product shots.
  • Starting the next clip's sound before its picture (J-cut).
  • Reusing a recorded voiceover in another project.

Getting an audio file out

This guide covers using audio inside the edit. HandCut exports video to Photos; it isn't an MP3 converter.

When this is useful

Extracting the audio is the tidy way to keep a voiceover or interview when you replace the picture, or to reuse a take under a new B-roll clip. It also lets you duck music under speech without touching the original video clip. After you extract, you can trim the audio independently, then export the project as usual. If you only need the sound file itself, share or save that audio clip after export. HandCut does not send the audio anywhere to extract it; the split happens on your iPhone.

Questions people ask

Does extracting change the original clip?

No. The original stays in Photos; the edit works on the project.

Can I remove noise from the extracted audio?

Yes, Reduce Noise (Pro) works on clips in the edit.
