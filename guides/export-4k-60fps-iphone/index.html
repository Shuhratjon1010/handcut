How to export 4K video at 60 fps on iPhone

Short answer: In HandCut's export sheet, choose 4K. The frame rate follows your footage up to 60 fps, 4K files use HEVC, and nothing is stamped on the video: there's no watermark on any export.

Settings that matter

  • Resolution: up to 4K (3840×2160). The sheet only says 4K when 4K is what's being written.
  • Frame rate: follows your source, up to 60 fps. Exporting 30 fps footage at 60 doesn't add real frames, and HandCut tells you so instead of silently doing it.
  • Codec: HEVC at 4K for smaller files; H.264 at 1080p and below for maximum compatibility.

When 1080p is the better pick

TikTok, Reels and Shorts recompress uploads, and most people watch on a phone. 1080p uploads faster and looks the same in the feed. Export 4K for YouTube, client deliverables, or when you'll crop later.

File size

A minute of 4K60 can be hundreds of megabytes. Keep free space on the iPhone before exporting; if an export fails, HandCut cleans up the partial file.

No watermark

HandCut never adds a logo or ending clip. What you see in the preview is what's exported.

4K isn't a separate upgrade. For new users, any export needs the monthly plan, and 4K is included.

No. Export is capped at 60 fps.
