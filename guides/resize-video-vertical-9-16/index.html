How to change a video's aspect ratio on iPhone

Short answer: In HandCut, open Canvas and pick 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 or 16:9. Then either fill the frame and reposition, or keep the whole picture and add a blurred background instead of black bars. Pro's Auto-Reframe can follow the speaker for you.

Step by step

  1. Open Canvas. Import your clip, then tap Canvas.
  2. Pick the shape. 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts; 4:5 for the Instagram feed; 1:1 for square; 16:9 for YouTube.
  3. Fit or fill. Fill crops to the new shape and you drag to reposition. Fit keeps everything and leaves space at the edges.
  4. Fill the edges. If you chose fit, add a blurred background so the empty space isn't plain black.
  5. Export. Tap Export Video.

Turning a horizontal clip vertical

Cropping 16:9 to 9:16 keeps only about a third of the width, so the subject must stay in that slice. Auto-Reframe moves the crop to keep the person in frame.

Questions people ask

Will resizing lower the quality?

Cropping uses fewer of the original pixels. Start from 4K if you plan to crop a horizontal video to vertical.

Can I mirror or rotate too?

Yes, mirror and crop are in the editor.
