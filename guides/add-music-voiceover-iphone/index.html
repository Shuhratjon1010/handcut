How to add music and a voiceover that ducks automatically

Short answer: In HandCut's Music & Voiceover mixer, record a voiceover and add a music track. Ducking lowers the music under your words, following your caption word timings and voiceovers, so speech stays clear.

Step by step

  1. Record the voiceover. Open the audio mixer and record a voiceover while the video plays.
  2. Add music. Import a music file onto the timeline.
  3. Turn on ducking. Ducking follows your words, so generate captions or record a voiceover first.
  4. Set levels. Adjust voiceover and music volume. Fade the music in and out at the ends.
  5. Export. Tap Export Video.

Other audio tools

  • Text to Speech: generate a voice from typed text.
  • Voice Changer: change the character of a voice.
  • Extract Audio: pull the sound out of a clip.
  • Reduce Noise: clean up a noisy recording (Pro).

Why ducking beats one fixed volume

Music loud enough to feel good between sentences buries the words while you talk. Ducking moves the level for you.

Troubleshooting

  • "No audible audio on the timeline": a clip is muted. Unmute it or add a voiceover.
  • Voiceover muted in the editor: check the voiceover volume in the mixer.
  • Music still too loud: lower the music track itself; ducking reduces it further under speech.

Questions people ask

Why does HandCut say there's nothing to duck?

Ducking follows speech. Generate captions or record a voiceover first so it knows where the words are.

Can I mute the original clip audio?

Yes, in the mixer.
