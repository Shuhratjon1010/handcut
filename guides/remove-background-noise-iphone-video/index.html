How to reduce background noise in an iPhone video

Short answer: Select the clip in HandCut and turn on Reduce Noise (denoise is a Pro tool). It lowers steady background sound like hum, fans and air conditioning so speech is clearer, which also makes captions more accurate.

Step by step

  1. Select the clip. Tap the clip on the timeline and open its audio settings.
  2. Turn on Reduce Noise. Toggle Reduce Noise and play it back.
  3. Balance music. If you add music, let ducking lower it under your voice.
  4. Regenerate captions if needed. Cleaner audio can improve recognition, so regenerate captions after denoising if there were many mistakes.

What noise reduction can and can't fix

Steady noise (fans, hum, hiss) reduces well. Sudden noise (dogs, traffic horns, other voices) is much harder. Heavy echo in an empty room isn't noise and won't go away.

Recording tips that beat any filter

  • Get the mic close: a wired or wireless lav, or the phone at arm's length.
  • Record in a room with soft furniture.
  • Turn off fans and air conditioning for the take.

Troubleshooting

  • Voice sounds thin or robotic: noise reduction removed too much. Record closer to the mic next time.
  • Noise in only one clip: turn Reduce Noise on for that clip only.
  • Music sounds odd: noise reduction is meant for speech. Keep music on its own track.

Questions people ask

Is there a free noise remover on iPhone?

iOS has a Voice Isolation mic mode for calls and some apps, which can help while recording. HandCut's Reduce Noise works on video you already recorded.

Does denoise run offline?
