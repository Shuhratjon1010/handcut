How to make captions offline, with no internet

Short answer: HandCut's captions use Apple's on-device speech recognition only, with no server fallback, so Generate Captions works in airplane mode and your audio never leaves the iPhone.

What works offline

  • Generating and editing captions
  • Styling, animation and the whole editor
  • Exporting to Photos (once your plan is active)
  • Caption translation, if the language is already downloaded in iOS

What needs a connection

  • Downloading the app and buying or restoring the plan
  • Downloading a new iOS speech or translation language
  • Uploading to TikTok, Instagram or YouTube

Why on-device matters

Client calls, coaching sessions, patients, kids: some recordings shouldn't go to a server. On-device captions keep them on your phone. The App Store privacy label for HandCut says data is not collected.

If your language isn't available

HandCut's captions are built primarily for English speech. Other languages work only when your iPhone has an on-device speech model for them, and the app tells you before you generate if yours does not. iOS may offer to download the on-device model in Settings.

Check it yourself

Turn on Airplane Mode, open a project and tap Generate Captions. If your language's on-device model is installed, captions appear without a connection. That's the simplest proof that the audio isn't going anywhere.

Questions people ask

Do on-device captions work in any language?

They depend on the on-device speech models iOS has for your language. English is the primary supported language.

Is on-device less accurate than cloud captions?

On clear speech, Apple's on-device recognition is strong. Cloud services may handle more languages and heavy accents better.
