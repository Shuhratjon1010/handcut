Apple Clips alternatives for automatic captions

Short answer: Apple says Clips is no longer updated and hasn't been available to new users since 10 October 2025. If you used its Live Titles to caption speech, you need another app. HandCut generates word-timed captions on the iPhone, like Live Titles did, and adds a full timeline.

What Apple says

Apple's support page says Clips will no longer be updated, isn't available to download for new users as of 10 October 2025, and can still be used on iOS 26 and iPadOS 26 or earlier. If you downloaded it before, you can redownload it from your purchase history. Apple recommends saving your Clips videos to Photos, as videos rather than projects.

Save your Clips videos first

  1. Open each Clips project.
  2. Save it as a video (with effects) to your Photos library.
  3. Keep the originals too if you might re-edit.

Replacing Live Titles

Live Titles put your words on screen as you recorded. In HandCut you record first (or use Teleprompter), then tap Generate Captions. Captions are made on the iPhone, every word keeps its timing, and you can edit, restyle and move them afterwards, which Live Titles made hard.

HandCut's captions are built primarily for English speech. Other languages work only when your iPhone has an on-device speech model for them, and the app tells you before you generate if yours does not.

Other options

Apple's page also names iMovie, InShot, VN Video Editor and GoPro Quik for making new videos. iMovie has typed titles but no automatic captions. See our alternatives roundup and caption apps compared.

Questions people ask

Can I still use Clips?

Apple says you can keep using it on iOS 26 and iPadOS 26 or earlier, and redownload it if you had it before.

Can HandCut open Clips projects?

No. Save Clips videos to Photos first, then import the videos.
