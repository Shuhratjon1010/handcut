How to add captions to Instagram Reels

Short answer: Instagram's caption sticker is quick but limited in style and timing. To get burned-in, word-by-word captions on a Reel, edit the clip in HandCut first: generate captions, pick a style, place them mid-frame, export in 9:16 and upload to Instagram.

Step by step

  1. Start with the Reel's video. Record in the Camera app or import an existing clip with Add Video. Use Canvas to set 9:16.
  2. Generate captions. Tap Generate Captions. The transcription happens on your iPhone.
  3. Edit the words. Tap a caption to fix names, hashtags you say out loud, or slang.
  4. Style for Reels. Choose a style and animation in Style. If a word matters, Pro's Emoji on key words can glue one emoji to it.
  5. Export. Tap Export Video and upload the saved file in Instagram. Leave Instagram's own caption sticker off.

Where to put captions on a Reel

Instagram shows your username, the post caption and the audio label along the bottom, and buttons down the right side. Text in the middle third of the frame stays readable. Our safe-zone guide covers this for each platform.

Why burn captions in for Reels

Burned-in captions look the same when the Reel is shared to Stories, sent in a DM or reposted on another app. They also let you use a look that matches your brand instead of the default sticker.

Questions people ask

Does Instagram have auto captions?

Yes, Instagram offers a captions sticker and auto-generated captions in some regions. They're easy but give you less control over timing and style than burning captions in.

Can I make Reels captions without internet?

Yes. HandCut's captions are made on the iPhone and need no connection. Uploading the Reel needs internet, of course.

Can I use the same video on TikTok and Shorts?

Yes. A 9:16 export with burned-in captions works on all three. Check the safe zones for each.
