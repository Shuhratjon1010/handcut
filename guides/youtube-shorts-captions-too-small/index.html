YouTube Shorts captions too small: how to make them bigger

Short answer: YouTube's CC track size is controlled by each viewer's caption settings, not by you. To make captions big for everyone, burn them into the Short in your own size and style before uploading, and keep them mid-frame so the Shorts interface doesn't cut them off.

Why CC captions look small

Closed captions are drawn by the YouTube player using the viewer's preferences. Defaults are designed for long videos on big screens, so on a vertical phone video they look small, and the Shorts title and buttons can overlap them.

Burn in big captions

  1. Import the Short in HandCut and set Canvas to 9:16.
  2. Tap Generate Captions.
  3. In Style, choose a bold look and fewer words per caption. Bigger text needs shorter lines.
  4. Drag captions to the middle band.
  5. Export Video and upload.

Cut off at the edges?

Text near the edges gets clipped on some phones and hidden by the Shorts overlay. Keep a margin on both sides and avoid the bottom fifth.

Keep CC too

You can still upload an .srt for viewers who use CC. See exporting an SRT.

Questions people ask

Can I change YouTube's caption size for my viewers?

No. Viewers set CC size in their own settings.

Do burned-in captions hurt search?

YouTube can't read burned-in text as captions. Upload an SRT as well if you want the words as a text track.
