How to add captions to Instagram Stories

Short answer: For a Story where you talk to camera, record or import the clip in HandCut, set the canvas to 9:16, generate captions and place them above the reply bar at the bottom, export, then post it as a Story from Photos.

Step by step

  1. Record or import. Use the Camera app, Teleprompter, or Add Video for an existing clip.
  2. Set 9:16. Open Canvas and choose 9:16, the Story shape.
  3. Generate captions. Tap Generate Captions and fix any words.
  4. Mind the Story layout. Your profile picture and name sit at the top, and the reply bar and stickers at the bottom. Keep captions in the middle band.
  5. Export and post. Tap Export Video, then add the video from Photos in Instagram's Story camera.

Stories vs Reels captions

Stories disappear after 24 hours and are watched tap by tap, so lines need to be even shorter than on Reels. One idea per Story frame works well: split a long take into several 15-second Stories.

Keep it readable

  • Two lines at most.
  • High-contrast look with an outline or background bar.
  • Leave room if you'll add a poll or link sticker.

Questions people ask

Does Instagram caption Stories automatically?

Instagram has a captions sticker in some regions. Burning captions in gives you control over style and timing, and they stay when the Story is shared.

How long can the video be?

Instagram splits long Story videos into segments. Shorter clips are easier to caption and watch.
