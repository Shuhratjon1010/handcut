How to blur or censor a moving face in a video on iPhone

Short answer: Use HandCut's Censor with a Mosaic or Solid look, and attach it to motion tracking (Pro) so it follows the face, plate or screen as it moves. Tracking runs on the iPhone.

Step by step

  1. Choose Censor. Select the clip and open Censor.
  2. Pick a look. Mosaic pixelates; Solid covers with a block. Adjust opacity and feather.
  3. Track the subject. Tap the subject to track it. HandCut follows it through the clip.
  4. Check every frame where it matters. If tracking loses the subject (fast motion, the face turns away), re-track from that moment or trim the section.
  5. Export. Tap Export Video.

Why tracking matters

A fixed blur box works only if nothing moves. Tracking keeps the cover on the subject, and HandCut warns you when tracking was lost so a face isn't left uncovered.

Privacy

Blurring bystanders, children, addresses and licence plates is good practice before you post. The footage stays on your phone while you do it.

Troubleshooting

  • "Tracking lost": the subject left the frame or turned away. Adjust or re-track from that moment.
  • Cover too small: resize the censor region and increase feather for a softer edge.
  • Several faces: add one tracked censor per face.
