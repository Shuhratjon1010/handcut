How to add a facecam bubble over a screen recording

Short answer: Import the screen recording as the main clip, add your camera clip as an overlay, and use picture-in-picture to shape it into a facecam bubble you can move and resize. Generate captions from your voice and export, all in HandCut.

Step by step

  1. Record both parts. Record your screen with iOS Screen Recording, and record yourself with the front camera (or in one take using a second device).
  2. Import the screen recording. Tap Add Video and pick it as the base clip.
  3. Add the facecam. Add the camera clip as an overlay and set it to a picture-in-picture bubble.
  4. Position it. Drag the bubble to a corner that doesn't hide what you're demonstrating. Keyframes can move it if the important part of the screen moves.
  5. Caption and export. Generate captions from your voice track and tap Export Video.

Good uses

  • App walkthroughs and product demos
  • Reacting to a post or a chart
  • Explaining a spreadsheet or a slide
  • Customer support how-tos

Keep the sound in sync

If you recorded separately, start both recordings with a clap so you can line the clips up on the timeline.

Questions people ask

Can I record the facecam and screen at the same time on one iPhone?

iOS screen recording can capture the microphone but not the front camera overlay, so most people record the facecam separately and combine them in the editor.

Does the bubble count against a free tier?

Picture-in-picture is part of the editor. For new users, exporting needs the monthly plan.
