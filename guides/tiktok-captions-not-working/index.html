TikTok captions not working: what to check

Short answer: TikTok's own captions depend on its settings, your language and the viewer's settings, so they can be missing or hidden. Check that captions were turned on when you posted and that viewers haven't switched them off. If you need captions that always show, burn them into the video before you upload.

Common reasons TikTok captions don't show

  • Not turned on at posting: auto captions are an option on the post screen.
  • Viewer setting: viewers can hide captions, so you may see them while others don't.
  • Language: auto captions aren't available for every language.
  • Audio: music over speech, or very quiet speech, can leave captions empty or wrong.
  • App version: features move between updates. Update TikTok and check its in-app help.

Captions that can't go missing

Burned-in captions are part of the picture, so no setting can hide them. In HandCut: import, Generate Captions, fix words, Export Video, then upload. Turn off TikTok's own captions so viewers don't see two sets. Full steps are in adding auto captions to TikTok videos.

Questions people ask

Why are my TikTok captions wrong?

Speech recognition struggles with music, crosstalk and names. With burned-in captions you fix every word before posting.

Can I change burned-in captions after posting?

No. Burned-in captions are part of the video, so fix them before you upload, or re-upload a corrected export.
