How to add captions in iMovie on iPhone

Short answer: iMovie for iPhone doesn't generate captions from speech. Its App Store listing describes animated title styles that you type yourself. To caption a talking video, either type titles clip by clip in iMovie, or export from iMovie and generate word-timed captions in another app, such as HandCut.

What iMovie can do

iMovie's listing (version 3.0.6, read 18 November 2026) describes 11 animated title styles such as Split, Reveal and Pop-up, with control over font, colour, size and position. These are titles: you type each one and set where it appears. Nothing listens to the audio.

Option 1: titles by hand in iMovie

  1. Split the clip at each sentence.
  2. Add a title to each piece and type what's said.
  3. Keep each title to one short line.

Fine for a 15-second clip with three sentences. For anything longer it's slow, and the text changes per clip, not per word.

Option 2: edit in iMovie, caption the export

  1. Finish your edit in iMovie and save the video to Photos.
  2. Open it in HandCut with Add Video and tap Generate Captions.
  3. Fix any words, choose a style, and tap Export Video.

Captions are made on the iPhone and timed to each word, and there's no watermark. Generate captions on the final edit so the timing matches.

Option 3: do the whole edit in one app

If captions matter on most of your videos, editing in an app with built-in captions saves a step. HandCut has a multi-clip timeline, transitions, music and voiceover, so short talking videos can be cut and captioned in one place.
