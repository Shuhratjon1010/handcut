How to use green screen (chroma key) on iPhone

Short answer: Put your green-screen clip on a layer above a background clip or image, select it, open Chroma key, and pick the screen colour with the eyedropper. HandCut removes it so the background shows through. Without a green screen, Smart Cutout (Pro) can cut out a person instead.

Step by step

  1. Add the background first. Import the clip or image that should sit behind you.
  2. Add your green-screen clip as an overlay. Place it on the layer above.
  3. Open Chroma key. Select the overlay and choose Chroma key.
  4. Pick the colour. Use the eyedropper on the green (or a preset), then tune the strength until the edges are clean.
  5. Caption and export. Generate captions from your voice and export.

Clean edges start at recording

  • Light the screen evenly; shadows become a different green.
  • Stand a metre or more from the screen to cut green spill on your hair and shoulders.
  • Don't wear green.

No green screen?

Smart Cutout uses on-device person segmentation to separate you from any background. It's a Pro tool and works best with a clear outline and good light.
