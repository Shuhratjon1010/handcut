How to add a hook title to a short video

Short answer: After generating captions, use Hook title (Pro). HandCut takes the first thing you say, up to seven words, and shows it as a big title near the top for roughly one and a half to three seconds. It's plain text you can edit or move.

How Hook title works

  • It reads the first line of your captions.
  • It keeps it short, at most seven words, so it can be read in two seconds.
  • It sits below the app tabs and above your captions.
  • There's one per project; making a new one replaces the old.
  • It isn't a caption, so it doesn't end up in your transcript or SRT.

Write a hook worth showing

  • Lead with the outcome: "How I cut my editing time in half".
  • Ask the question the viewer already has: "Why do my captions get covered?"
  • Name who it's for: "Realtors, film this first".

If your first sentence isn't a good hook, re-record the opening or edit the title text.

Troubleshooting

  • Hook text is cut short: hooks are capped at seven words and about 44 characters so they can be read in time. Edit the text to shorten your idea.
  • The hook covers my face: drag it lower or higher in the Text tray.
  • I made a new hook and the old one disappeared: there's one hook per project by design.
