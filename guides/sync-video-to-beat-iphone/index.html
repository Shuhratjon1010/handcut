How to sync clips to the beat on iPhone

Short answer: Add your music, detect beats to place beat markers, then use Auto-Beat Sync to cut the clips on those markers. You can also add markers by hand and snap cuts to them.

Step by step

  1. Add clips and music. Import several clips, then add a music track.
  2. Detect beats. Run beat detection. HandCut places beat markers on the timeline.
  3. Auto-Beat Sync. Use Auto-Beat Sync to cut the clips to the detected beats. If the clips already line up, HandCut says so.
  4. Adjust. Drag cuts, delete a marker or add your own on accent beats.
  5. Export. Tap Export Video.

Make it feel intentional

  • Cut on every beat for energy, every other beat for a calmer feel.
  • Put your best shot on the drop.
  • Transitions like whip pan or zoom work well on a strong beat.

Music rights

Use music you have the rights to, or add the track inside TikTok or Instagram from their licensed libraries after export.

Troubleshooting

  • "Detect beats first before cutting": run beat detection on the music before Auto-Beat Sync.
  • Too many cuts: delete markers on weak beats.
  • Clips too short for transitions: use hard cuts on fast beats and keep transitions for the bigger moments.
