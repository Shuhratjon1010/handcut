How to stabilize shaky video on iPhone

Short answer: Select the clip in HandCut and choose Stabilize Video, then pick Standard, Cinema or Ultra. Stronger modes smooth more shake but crop in more. Check the result, and if a clip can't be stabilized, HandCut tells you.

Step by step

  1. Select the clip. Tap it on the timeline and open the adjust tools.
  2. Stabilize. Choose Stabilize Video.
  3. Pick a mode. Standard for light shake, Cinema for walking shots, Ultra for heavy shake.
  4. Compare. Play it back. Turn stabilization off if the crop cuts off something important.
  5. Export. Tap Export Video.

The trade-off

Stabilization moves and crops each frame to cancel motion, so stronger smoothing means a tighter frame. Recording in 4K gives you room to crop and still export a sharp 1080p.

Prevent shake while filming

  • Use the Camera app's Action mode on iPhones that have it.
  • Hold the phone with both hands, elbows in.
  • Walk heel to toe, slowly.

Questions people ask

What if HandCut says the clip could not be stabilized?

Try a different mode, or trim the clip to the shaky part and stabilize that.

Does it work offline?

Yes, on the iPhone.
