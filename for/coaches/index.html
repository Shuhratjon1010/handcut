Captioned short videos for coaches

Short answer: Coaches post the same kind of video every week: one person, one idea, talking to camera. HandCut is built for that: teleprompter, automatic silence removal, word-by-word captions made on the iPhone, and exports with no watermark.

A 10-minute weekly workflow

  1. Write three short tips. One idea each, under a minute spoken.
  2. Record them in Teleprompter, one take each.
  3. Run Auto-Cut to remove pauses.
  4. Generate Captions, fix names, pick your brand look once and reuse it.
  5. Add a Hook title with the promise of the tip.
  6. Export 9:16 and post to Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

Client privacy

Session clips and testimonials can be sensitive. HandCut makes captions on the phone with no server fallback and no account, and its App Store privacy label says data is not collected. You can also blur a face with tracked Censor.

What it doesn't do

HandCut has no template marketplace, stock library or scheduling. It's an editor; you post from your social apps.

Ideas that work for coaches

  • One mistake your clients make, and the fix.
  • A question from a client, answered in 40 seconds.
  • Before and after: what changed and why.
  • A myth in your field, and what's actually true.

Keep one look for captions and hook titles so viewers recognise your videos before they read them.
