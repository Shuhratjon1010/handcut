Captions for online course creators

Short answer: Course platforms want a caption file and students want readable captions. From one HandCut project you can export the video with captions burned in and an .srt file for the platform's caption track, and add a facecam bubble over screen recordings.

Lesson workflow

  1. Record the screen with iOS Screen Recording and yourself with the front camera.
  2. Combine them with a facecam bubble.
  3. Generate Captions and fix technical terms with find and replace.
  4. Export the video, then Export .srt from the caption tray.
  5. Upload both to your course platform.

Accessibility is a selling point

Captions help students who are deaf or hard of hearing, studying in a second language, or watching on a train. A caption file also lets platforms show searchable transcripts.

Long lessons

HandCut is an iPhone editor aimed at short and mid-length videos. For multi-hour courses edited on a computer, a desktop editor is the better tool; you can still use HandCut for promo clips and short lessons.

Questions people ask

Which caption format do course platforms take?

Most accept SRT; some prefer VTT, which is easy to convert from SRT.

Can I caption in Spanish or Portuguese?

HandCut's captions are built primarily for English speech. Other languages work only when your iPhone has an on-device speech model for them, and the app tells you before you generate if yours does not. You can also import an SRT made elsewhere.
