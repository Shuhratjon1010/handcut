Captioned product videos for small businesses

Short answer: Show the product, say why it matters, and caption it so it works with the sound off. HandCut covers the pieces a small shop needs: word-by-word captions, a facecam bubble for app or site demos, green screen, music with ducking, and exports with no watermark.

Three formats to start with

  • 15-second demo: hands and product, captions saying the one benefit.
  • Founder talk: you to camera, teleprompter, word-by-word captions.
  • How-to: screen recording of your store or app with a facecam bubble.

Captions sell on mute

Most feed video starts muted. If the benefit isn't on screen in the first seconds, many viewers never hear it. Put it in a hook title and the captions.

Keep claims honest

Platform ad rules and consumer law apply to what your captions say. Avoid claims you can't back up.

A simple weekly plan

Pick one product a week. Film a 15-second demo on Monday, a founder talk on Wednesday and a customer question answered on Friday. Reuse one caption look so your videos are recognisable in the feed. Batch the editing: one session in HandCut can caption all three.

Check what worked in each platform's analytics and keep the formats that hold viewers past three seconds.
