Captioned listing and market videos for realtors

Short answer: Agents film on their phone between showings. HandCut turns that footage into captioned, vertical videos without a laptop: walkthrough clips with transitions, a talking-head market update with word-by-word captions, and tracked blur for house numbers, plates and faces.

Two videos that work

  • Listing tour: 8–12 short clips, room by room, cut to music with Auto-Beat Sync, with a caption per room.
  • Market update: 45 seconds to camera with the teleprompter, word-by-word captions and a hook title.

Before you post

  • Blur neighbours' house numbers, licence plates and people with Censor and tracking.
  • Check your brokerage's rules for advertising and disclosures.
  • Keep captions mid-frame so platform buttons don't cover them.

Brokerage branding

There's no watermark from HandCut, so the only branding on the video is yours.

Filming tips for listings

  • Shoot vertical for social, horizontal for the listing website; or shoot 4K horizontal and crop.
  • Walk slowly and hold each room for three seconds so you have room to cut.
  • Turn on every light and open the blinds.
  • Record the voiceover afterwards in a quiet car, then add it in the mixer.

Questions people ask

Can I export for YouTube and Reels from the same edit?

Yes. Change the canvas from 16:9 to 9:16 and export again.

Can I use drone footage?

Yes, once it is in Photos it edits like any other clip. 360° video editing is not a feature.
