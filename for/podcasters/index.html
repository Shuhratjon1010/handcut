Captioned podcast clips from your iPhone

Short answer: Pick a strong 30–60 second moment, set the canvas to 9:16 and let Auto-Reframe keep the speaker in frame, remove silences, and generate word-by-word captions. HandCut does the whole clip on the phone.

Clip workflow

  1. Import the episode video (or the segment you want).
  2. Trim to one complete thought with a clear start.
  3. Canvas 9:16, then Auto-Reframe.
  4. Remove silences.
  5. Generate captions and add a hook title.
  6. Export and post.

Choosing the moment

The best clips answer one question or tell one story. Start the clip on the sentence that sets it up, not on "so, yeah".

Audio-only podcasts

HandCut is a video editor. For audio-only shows, put a still image or a recording of yourself on the timeline and caption the audio.

Common problems with podcast clips

  • Crosstalk: when two people speak at once, captions merge their words. Trim to one voice or fix the text by hand.
  • Room echo: echo lowers caption accuracy more than background hum. Use the cleanest mic track you have.
  • Wide shots: if both hosts sit in one wide frame, a 9:16 crop can only hold one. Cut between them clip by clip.
  • Long clips: under 60 seconds usually holds attention best on Shorts and Reels.

Questions people ask

Can it find the best moments for me?

No. HandCut doesn't pick clips automatically; you choose the moment.

Multiple speakers?

Captions follow all speech. Auto-Reframe follows a person, so split the clip by speaker for conversations.
