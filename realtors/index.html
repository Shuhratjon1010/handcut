Why it fits

Figures you can check

Prices, square footage and street names are exactly where speech recognition can slip. Tap the word in the transcript and correct it.

Sharp enough for the property

Export in up to 4K at up to 60fps where your iPhone supports it, with no watermark on the free plan.

Vertical from any shot, with Pro

Auto-Reframe and motion tracking help keep you or the room in frame, and Magic Edit trims filler words and long pauses from a walkthrough.

Captions depend on iOS having an on-device speech model for your language on your iPhone; English is the primary language. See Free and Pro for what each plan includes.

Questions

How do I add captions to a listing video on iPhone?

Open your walkthrough clip, generate captions on your iPhone, correct any address, price or name in the transcript, choose a caption look and export up to 4K with no watermark.

Can I read a script while I film a market update?

Yes. The teleprompter camera scrolls your script near the lens while you record, and it is free.
