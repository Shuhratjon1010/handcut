A video editor for iPhone with no watermark

HandCut never stamps your video. There is no logo in the corner and no branded clip at the end, whatever you make.

Edit free. Export with Pro, a monthly subscription. Price shown in the app.

What you can do

  • Word-by-word auto captions made on your iPhone
  • Multi-clip timeline: trim, split, reorder, 14 transitions, speed 0.25x to 4x
  • Text, emoji and your own images; manual keyframes; mirror, crop and reframe
  • Music and voiceover tracks with a mixer; picture-in-picture facecam
  • Export up to 4K, up to 60 fps, with no watermark

Honest about the price

You can download HandCut and edit for free. Exporting needs HandCut Pro, a monthly subscription. The App Store price for your country is shown in the app before you buy, and you can cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Privacy and languages

The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. Captions are built for English; other languages work only where your iPhone's on-device speech model is installed. The app interface is in English. See also the longer no-watermark comparison and the guides.

FAQ

Does HandCut ever add a watermark?

No. There is no watermark on any export, and no branded end clip.

Is it a free video editor?

Editing is free: import, captions, timeline, styles and keyframes. Exporting needs a HandCut Pro monthly subscription; the price is shown in the app before you buy.

What resolution can I export?

Up to 4K, at up to 60 fps, following your footage.
